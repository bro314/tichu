## 1. Why & Goal

Players at a live or turn-based Tichu table currently hit four avoidable table-HUD gaps:

1. **Invisible Phoenix Value ([#161585](https://boardgamearena.com/bug?id=161585))**: Once earlier players pass (replacing their combo slot with a Pass card) or a player refreshes, neither `<tichu-last-played>` nor `<tichu-trick-modal>` displays what numeric/rank value Phoenix represents (`1.5`, `9.5`, `Q.5`, or `= J` in a Straight/Full House/Pair/Stairs).
2. **Hidden Round Counter During Card Exchange ([#52829](https://boardgamearena.com/bug?id=52829))**: `<tichu-status>` lives inside `#card-last-played-area`, which sets `display: none` whenever `isPassState` is true, hiding the Round indicator throughout `giveCards`, `showPassedCards`, and `acceptPassedCards`.
3. **Misleading `"Played Cards"` Heading ([#99318](https://boardgamearena.com/bug?id=99318))**: Turn-based mode's card tracker hides `.trick` and `.captured` cards and dims `.hand`, leaving _unplayed_ cards visible—yet titles the panel `"Played Cards"`.
4. **Missing Last Completed Trick & Player Aid (`?`)**: `<tichu-status>` only exposes `"Show current trick"` (rendered in `<tichu-trick-modal>` with stale `#popin_showLastTrickDialog` DOM IDs and fixed `540px` desktop width), and players have no in-table quick reference (`?`) for special cards, bomb hierarchy, or Tichu/Grand Tichu scoring.

## 3. Architectural Decisions & Trade-offs

1. **Where to render the realized Phoenix badge (`<tichu-card>` corner overlay vs per-seat text header)**:
   - **Recommended**: Add `@property({ type: Number }) phoenixValue: number | null = null` on `<tichu-stock>` and `<tichu-card>` (passed **only** from `<tichu-last-played>` and `<tichu-trick-modal>`, keeping `null` in hand, pass slots, and `<tichu-replay>`). Inside `<tichu-card>`'s `div#card`, render a high-contrast pill at `top: 4px; left: 4px;` when the card is Phoenix (`type === 2 && type_arg === 1`) and `phoenixValue !== null`. Because `<tichu-stock>` overlaps cards from left to right (leaving each card's **left** edge exposed in Pairs, Triples, middle Straights, and `AABB` Full Houses) and `getCardRects()` measures `#card`'s bounding box, a top-left pill inside `#card` stays visible across all combos and never perturbs WAAPI flight `DOMRect`s.
   - _Rejected alternative_: External overlay in `<tichu-last-played>` only — duplicates badge logic in `<tichu-trick-modal>` and fails to stay anchored to the exact Phoenix card inside multi-card combos.
2. **Where to lift `<tichu-status>` (`<tichu-game>` HUD bar vs inside `<tichu-last-played>` sibling)**:
   - **Recommended**: Move `<tichu-status>` directly into [`TichuGame::render()`](file:///Users/brohlfs/git/tichu/src/elements/tichu-game.ts#L153-L180) above `<tichu-value-choice>`, `<tichu-last-played>`, and `<tichu-pass-area>`, styled as a persistent flex-wrap HUD strip (`min-height: 44px`, compact `Round`, `Trick`, `Trick Points`, and action buttons). Keep `"Current trick"` and `"Last trick"` rendered as `disabled` pills (with explanatory `title`) rather than `display: none` so the bar never shifts height across tricks.
   - _Rejected alternative_: Duplicating a second round label inside `<tichu-pass-area>` — still leaves `grandTichuBets` / transitions inconsistent and fragments Plan 10's `"Scores"` and Plan 11's `"?"` entry points.
3. **Source of truth for `currentTrickPlays` and `lastCompletedTrick` across `F5` (existing `actionlog` vs new DB columns)**:
   - **Recommended**: Derive both strictly from existing `actionlog` rows (`combo`, `confirm`, `chooseDragonGift`) scoped to current `log_round` inside `LogManager`, paired with live client tracking in `GameModel::applyPlayCombo()` + `GameModel::applyCaptureCards()` (where `prev.lastComboPlayerId` is still the trick winner and `playerId` is the card recipient!). Restrict `"Last trick"` to the most recent completed trick of the _current round_ (`null` on Trick 1 or during pass/GT states), matching `Coinche` / `The Crew` in [`BGA_OPEN_SOURCE_ECOSYSTEM.md`](file:///Users/brohlfs/git/tichu/docs/BGA_OPEN_SOURCE_ECOSYSTEM.md#L76-L78).
   - _Rejected alternative_: Client-only accumulation (wipes on `F5`) or restricting `"Last trick"` to `isAllInfoExposed` (hides an expected convenience in real-time games; restricting to just the single immediately preceding trick preserves trick-memory skill).
4. **Floating `<tichu-help>` modal pattern**:
   - **Recommended**: Native Lit Shadow DOM `<tichu-help>` mounted on `document.body` (mirroring `<tichu-trick-modal>` and following [`BGA_OPEN_SOURCE_ECOSYSTEM.md`](file:///Users/brohlfs/git/tichu/docs/BGA_OPEN_SOURCE_ECOSYSTEM.md#L114) instead of UMD `thoun/bga-help`), immune to `rescale()` CSS `transform`, supporting `Escape` close and `:host-context(html[data-theme="dark"])`.

## 4. 4-Way Live-Game Compatibility & Concurrency Audit

1. **Direction 1 — `OLD DB -> NEW PHP + NEW JS`**: Zero `dbmodel.sql` or `upgradeTableDb()` changes, zero new globals or `ST_*` states. `LogManager` reads existing `combo`/`confirm`/`chooseDragonGift` rows with defensive null-coalescing (`$arg['phoenixValue'] ?? null`, fallback winner from last `combo` row if a legacy row lacked `confirm`).
2. **Direction 2 — `OLD open JS tab <-> NEW PHP`**: `tichu.action.php` and `NotificationManager.php` are unchanged (`playCombo` already emits `type` and `phoenixValue`; `captureCards` already emits `player_id` and `trick_value`). Unknown additive keys (`currentTrickPlays`, `lastCompletedTrick`) in `getAllDatas()` are harmlessly ignored by any already-open old tab.
3. **Direction 3 — `NEW open JS tab <-> OLD PHP` (rollback)**: `GameModel::hydrateFromGamedatas()` treats `currentTrickPlays` and `lastCompletedTrick` as optional (`undefined` -> falls back to `trickLastCombo.phoenixValue` for single-Phoenix tricks, keeps `lastCompletedTrick = null` until next live `captureCards` notification).
4. **Direction 4 — `NEW DB / actionlog -> OLD PHP + OLD JS` (rollback)**: Plan 11 performs **zero** new DB writes, globals, or `actionlog` insertions, so DB state created by `NEW PHP` is 100% identical to `OLD PHP`.

- **Concurrency & Locking Invariants**: Read-only `getAllDatas()` / `LogManager` queries strictly **do not** call `acquireGameLock()`, preserving `tichu-concurrency-locks` rules; no mutating endpoints or state transitions change (`@thoun` `player_pass` autopass resets untouched).
- **`<tichu-replay>` & `import-bug-sql.php` & Sandbox Safety**: `<tichu-card>`/`<tichu-stock>` default `phoenixValue` to `null` so `<tichu-replay>` (`<tichu-player>`) is unaffected; `import-bug-sql.php` Surface 4 already remaps `actionlog.log_player` and `chooseDragonGift.recipient`; `LogManager` uses only `Table::getObjectListFromDB` / `Table::getUniqueValueFromDB` (100% `npm run test:contracts` compliant).

## 5. Step-by-Step Implementation Blueprint

1. **Shared Phoenix Formatting Utility (`src/elements/combo-validator.ts`)**:
   - Add pure helpers `formatShortRank(rank: number)` (`2..10`, `11->"J"`, `12->"Q"`, `13->"K"`, `14->"A"`) and `formatPhoenixBadge(phoenixValue: number | number[] | null | undefined): string`: returns `"1.5"` for `1.5`, `"<rank>.5"` for other `.5` singles (`"9.5"`, `"Q.5"`, `"A.5"`), `"= <rank>"` for integer combo substitutions (`"= 7"`, `"= J"`), and `""` otherwise.
2. **Phoenix Value Badge on `<tichu-card>` & `<tichu-stock>` (`src/elements/tichu-card.ts`, `src/elements/tichu-stock.ts`)**:
   - In `TichuCard`: add `@property({ type: Number }) phoenixValue: number | null = null`. When `phoenixValue !== null` on Phoenix (`type === 2 && type_arg === 1`), compute `badge = formatPhoenixBadge(this.phoenixValue)` and render `<span class="phoenix-value-badge" aria-label=${...}>${badge}</span>` inside `div#card` (`position: absolute; top: 4px; left: 4px; z-index: 2; pointer-events: none;`, high-contrast `#1f2328` bg with `#fef08a` / `#fff` border+text so it stands out on standard & Tiki art without affecting `#card` rect). Enhance `getComputedTooltip()` using literal translated templates (`t("Phoenix played as ${value} (scores -25 points)").replace("${value}", badge)`). Never bind `style=` on `<tichu-card>` from parents.
   - In `TichuStock`: add `@property({ attribute: false }) phoenixValue: number | null = null` and pass `.phoenixValue=${Number(card.type) === 2 && Number(card.type_arg) === 1 ? this.phoenixValue : null}` on `<tichu-card>`.
   - In `TichuLastPlayed::renderPlayerArea()`: pass `.phoenixValue=${typeof combo.phoenixValue === "number" ? combo.phoenixValue : null}` on the player's `<tichu-stock>`.
3. **Server Trick History & `F5` Hydration (`modules/managers/LogManager.php`, `tichu.game.php`, `src/types/tichu-contracts.ts`)**:
   - Add `LogManager::getTrickPlaysAndSummary(int $round, int $dbTrick): array` querying `log_id, log_player, log_action, log_arg FROM actionlog WHERE log_round = $round AND log_trick = $dbTrick ORDER BY log_id ASC`, extracting ordered `combo` plays (each with normalized `phoenixValue`), flattened `cards`, `winnerId` (last `confirm.log_player`, fallback last `combo.log_player`), `recipientId` (`chooseDragonGift.arg['recipient']` if present, else `winnerId`), `points` (`chooseDragonGift.arg['trickValue']` or `CardManager::getTrickValue($cards)`), and `phoenixValue`.
   - Add `LogManager::getCurrentTrickPlays(): array` and `LogManager::getLastCompletedTrick(): ?array` (finds `MAX(log_trick) WHERE log_round = $round AND log_trick > 0 AND log_trick < $currentDbTrick`; returns `null` if `0` or `$currentDbTrick <= 0`).
   - In `Tichu::getAllDatas()`: add `"currentTrickPlays" => LogManager::getCurrentTrickPlays()` and `"lastCompletedTrick" => LogManager::getLastCompletedTrick()`. Update `TichuGamedatas` (optional fields) and `TrickPlayRecord` / `CompletedTrickSummary` in `src/types/tichu-contracts.ts`.
4. **Live `GameModel` & `src/tichu.ts` Wiring (`src/elements/game-model.ts`, `src/tichu.ts`)**:
   - Extend `LiveGameState` with `currentTrickPlays: TrickPlayRecord[]`, `lastCompletedTrick: CompletedTrickSummary | null`, `trickModalMode: "current" | "last"`, `helpModalOpen: boolean` (+ selectors `currentTrickPlays$`, `lastCompletedTrick$`, `trickModalMode$`, `helpModalOpen$`).
   - Hydrate `currentTrickPlays` and `lastCompletedTrick` in `hydrateFromGamedatas()` (with defensive `undefined` handling and numeric normalization of `phoenixValue`).
   - Append to `currentTrickPlays` in `applyPlayCombo()`; snapshot `lastCompletedTrick` inside `applyCaptureCards(recipientPlayerId, trickValue)` using `prev.lastComboPlayerId || prev.trickLastCombo.playerId || String(recipientPlayerId)` as `winnerId`; clear `currentTrickPlays` in `enterNewTrick()` and both in `enterNewRound()`/`enterEndRound()`.
   - Add `openTrickModal(mode: "current" | "last")` (keeping `setTrickModalOpen(open)`) and `setHelpModalOpen(open)`. Keep only `import type` in `src/tichu.ts`, and mount `<tichu-help>` on `document.body` inside `rehydrateModel()` (`!document.querySelector("tichu-help")`).
