## 1. Why & Goal

Three visual and accessibility gaps affect Tichu across Retina screens, small table plays, color-vision deficiency, and keyboard/screen-reader play:

1. **Blurry 1x Card Sprites & Small Table Cards ([#36611](https://boardgamearena.com/bug?id=36611))**: Both `img/tichu-cards.png` and `img/tiki-cards.png` are `1400x600` (`100x150px` per card at `1x`), rendering soft on modern 2x/3x displays, while `0.75x` (`75x112.5px`) cards in `<tichu-last-played>` and `40x60px` tiles in `<tichu-played-cards>` have tiny corner indices that are hard to scan around the table.
2. **Colorblind Suit Ambiguity**: Standard Tichu relies heavily on Black/Red/Blue/Green coloring across Swords, Stars, Pagodas, and Jade (`tichu.css` / `img/*-cards.png`), especially at small `0.75x`/`0.4x` scales.
3. **Zero Keyboard Navigation & Missing Card ARIA/Tooltips**: `getDefaultCardTooltip()` returns `""` for regular cards `2..4, 6..9, J, Q, A`, and `<tichu-card>` / `<tichu-stock>` expose no `role`, `aria-label`, `aria-pressed`, roving `tabindex`, or arrow/Space/Enter/Escape handlers.

Goal: Deliver sharp `2800x1200` (2x) sprites with zero sprite math breakage, crisp high-readability corner badges on small table cards (`<= 0.75x`), an instant zero-reload **Pref `108`** Colorblind Suit Symbols mode (`♠`, `★`, `⌂`/`♣`, `◆`), and full WAI-ARIA roving keyboard play.

## 3. Architectural Decisions & Trade-offs

1. **2x HiDPI Sprite Format & Asset Pipeline (`PNG` vs `WebP`)**:
   - **Recommended (Option A)**: Replace `img/tichu-cards.png` & `img/tiki-cards.png` in place with optimized `2800x1200` (`200x300px`/card) PNGs (edge-preserving `magick` upscale + subtle unsharp + crisp re-rendered corner rank/suit glyphs on normal cards, preserving original center/special artwork fidelity and licensing) and `100x150` 2x `*-icons-pass.png`, optimized cleanly (`pngquant`/palette-PNG or `oxipng`, target `< 2 MB` each). Because `TichuCard` (`--bg-width: ${14 * width}px`) and `TichuPlayedCards` (`calc(var(--w) * 14)`) scale relative to card display size, **zero sprite coordinate math changes**. Exact filenames avoid Studio CDN/WebP allowlist or `local/router.php` MIME issues.
   - **Option B**: Add `.webp` with `image-set(...)` fallback. Smaller bytes, but doubles asset maintenance and risks BGA Studio minified-CSS URL rewriting issues.
2. **Table Card Legibility (`<= 0.75x`) & Colorblind Suit Symbols (`Pref 108`)**:
   - **Recommended (Option A)**: Add a lightweight vector corner-badge overlay inside `TichuCard`'s Shadow DOM (`div#card`) using inline `<svg aria-hidden="true">` suit shapes — Swords `♠` (`#111827`), Stars `★` (`#c81e1e`), Pagodas `⌂`/`♣` (`#1d4ed8`), Jade `◆` (`#15803d`) — so OS/Safari never substitutes color emoji. When `colorblind = true` (`Pref 108 = 2`), render crisp high-contrast top-left/bottom-right corner rank+suit badges + subtle suit-patterned border cue on `<tichu-card>` and `<tichu-played-cards>`. Additionally, on small table cards (`scale <= 0.75`, e.g., `<tichu-last-played>` & `<tichu-trick-modal>`), render a clean high-legibility corner rank+suit pill (even in Standard mode when `scale <= 0.75`, compact and non-intrusive) instead of inflating `<tichu-last-played>` row heights above `heightMin = 800` (which would trigger `rescale()` zoom-out!). Strictly avoid `filter: drop-shadow(...)` for `.dj_safari` GPU safety (`bga-release-and-qa/SKILL.md`), and never bind `style=${...}` on `<tichu-card>` from parent templates (`AGENTS.md`).
   - **Option B**: Increase `<tichu-last-played>` card `.scale` from `0.75` to `1.0`. _Rejected_ — 4 stacked rows in List mode exceed `800px`, forcing `Tichu::rescale()` to zoom out the entire board.
3. **Colorblind State Propagation & Persistence (`Pref 108`)**:
   - **Recommended (Option A)**: Wire `Pref 108` (`1` = Standard [default], `2` = High-contrast suit symbols & corner pips, `needReload => false`) through `Tichu::readPrefs()` -> `GameModel.colorblindSuits$` -> parent components (`tichu-hand`, `tichu-last-played`, `tichu-pass-area`, `tichu-trick-modal`, `tichu-played-cards`, `tichu-animation-layer`) passing `.colorblind` to `<tichu-stock>` / `<tichu-card>`, plus a quick-toggle link in `<tichu-hand>` `#prefs` mirroring `tiki_art` (`persistUserPreference`). Keeping `TichuCard` driven by `@property({ type: Boolean, reflect: true }) colorblind = false` preserves pure unit testing and `<tichu-replay>` independence. Exemplars in [`docs/BGA_OPEN_SOURCE_ECOSYSTEM.md`](file:///Users/brohlfs/git/tichu/docs/BGA_OPEN_SOURCE_ECOSYSTEM.md): `Sea Salt & Paper` (colorblind symbols), `Calypso` (4-color deck pref), and `Odin` (roving keyboard hand focus).
4. **Shadow DOM Roving Focus & ARIA (`tichu-card.ts` & `tichu-stock.ts`)**:
   - **Recommended (Option A)**: Enable `delegatesFocus: true` on `TichuCard.shadowRootOptions` with `role=${this.selectable ? "button" : "img"}`, `aria-pressed`, non-empty localized `title` + `aria-label` on ALL 56 cards, and roving `tabindex` (`0` on one card, `-1` on others) managed by `<tichu-stock>` (`ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`/`Home`/`End`/`Space`/`Enter`/`Escape`, stopping propagation on handled keys so `<tichu-replay>` `document` listeners never misfire).
