## 1. Why & Goal

In physical Tichu, players who have placed their 3 face-down pass cards can pick them back up anytime before the exchange occurs — both to fix a misclick (#218304) and to rethink their pass if someone declares Tichu during the exchange (#31597). On BGA today, confirming in `ST_GIVE_CARDS` immediately locks the player out until all 4 players submit. Experienced players therefore delay passing just to watch for opponent/partner Tichu bets, hurting both async pacing and real-time table flow.

Additionally, once `ST_ACCEPT_PASSED_CARDS` moves received cards into the hand, all 3 incoming cards merge silently into the sorted hand with **zero visual marker or tooltip** indicating whether a card came from **Left**, **Partner**, or **Right** — even though `card.card_passed_from` stays populated in MySQL until the next round.

**Goal**:

1. Add serialized out-of-turn **`unpassCards`** (#218304) under `acquireGameLock()` so an already-submitted player can retract their 3 cards into an editable draft while `ST_GIVE_CARDS` remains active.
2. Inside **`tichuBet()`** during `ST_GIVE_CARDS` (#31597), atomically reopen all already-locked **non-zombie** passes, preserve zombies untouched, top up clock time, and pre-slot the players' previous 3 card IDs locally in `GameModel` with an inline banner so they can adjust or re-confirm in 1 click.
3. Expose private `passed_from` only to the hand owner (both live and on F5 reload) and render a 5-second arrival pulse plus subtle persistent `L` / `P` / `R` origin badge and sender tooltip inside `<tichu-hand>` only.

## 3. Architectural Decisions & Trade-offs

1. **Out-of-Turn `unpassCards` Entry Point & Lock Policy (#218304)**:
   - **Recommended**: Expose `unpassCards()` on [`action_tichu`](file:///Users/brohlfs/git/tichu/tichu.action.php) and `TichuActionMap` without adding it to `ST_GIVE_CARDS["possibleactions"]` and **without** calling `$this->checkAction('unpassCards')` (paralleling `cancelAutopass()` / `playBomb()`), because BGA `checkAction()` rejects inactive players. Inside `Tichu::unpassCards()`, call `$this->acquireGameLock()` (not `acquireGameLockOrFail()`): verify `$this->freshStateId === ST_GIVE_CARDS`, verify `!$this->isPlayerMultiactiveFresh($playerId)`, and require `3` cards with `card_location = 'temporary' AND card_passed_from = $playerId FOR UPDATE`. If the 4th passer already triggered `ST_ACCEPT_PASSED_CARDS` -> `ST_PLAY_COMBO_OPEN`, throw crisp `UserException(clienttranslate("All players have already passed their cards"))`.
   - **Why `acquireGameLock()` over `acquireGameLockOrFail()`**: If Player B passes concurrently while Player A clicks Undo Pass, `freshStateId` is still `ST_GIVE_CARDS`; succeeding without forcing a spurious retry error feels instant, while `bumpGameVersion()` still forces a concurrent 4th-player `giveTheCards()` (`setPlayerNonMultiactiveSafe(..., false)`) to throw/retry cleanly rather than advancing state with stale active counts. Follows the `7 Wonders` / `Sushi Go!` multiactive retract pattern ([`docs/BGA_OPEN_SOURCE_ECOSYSTEM.md`](file:///Users/brohlfs/git/tichu/docs/BGA_OPEN_SOURCE_ECOSYSTEM.md)).
   - **Alternative rejected**: Splitting passing into confirm-only stages or adding a new pre/post pass state, which would break 4-way live-game rollbacks and add unnecessary round clicks.

2. **Tichu-Triggered Pass Reopen, Zombie Exclusion & Timers (#31597)**:
   - **Recommended**: Inside `tichuBet()`, strictly **after** both `$confirmed` prompts (`L946-L967`) pass and `bumpGameVersion()` (`L968`) executes, when `$stateId === ST_GIVE_CARDS`, query `SELECT player_id, player_zombie FROM player WHERE player_id <> $player_id AND player_is_multiactive = 0 FOR UPDATE`. Filter out any `player_zombie == 1` rows (reopening a zombie triggers `ZombieStateException` in `checkReturnState()` because `setPlayersMultiactive()` omits `checkZombieTurn()`; keeping zombie passes locked in `'temporary'` preserves `zombiGiveCards()` and passes `race-tests.php#L1083`). Return each qualifying human's 3 `'temporary'` cards to `'hand'`, log `unpassCards` `{ "reopenedByTichu": true, "callerId": $player_id }`, send private `unpassCards` notifs, reactivate them via `setPlayersMultiactiveSafe()`, call `giveExtraTime($otherId)` in realtime, and emit a public `NotificationManager::log` notice (never exposing card values). Exclude `$player_id` itself if `$player_id` had already passed (they can already click **"Undo Pass"**), or note in review UX that callers still have undo access.
   - **Stale-Safe Reactivation Helper**: Implement `Tichu::setPlayersMultiactiveSafe(array $playerIds)` in `tichu.game.php` (never patch upstream `local/Bga/GameFramework/GamestateMachine.php`). Always call `$this->syncStaleReturnState()` **before** `giveExtraTime()` when `$this->gameLockStale` is true so `reflexionTimeUpdate()` and `checkActivePlayersChange()` see the authoritative active list.
   - **Alternative rejected**: Reopening passes unconditionally on the first click even when `confirmTichu` (`#80004` / 60s rule) aborts the call for confirmation.

3. **Pre-Slotted Editable Draft on Reopen & F5 Waiting Recovery**:
   - **Recommended**: In MySQL, returned cards **must** sit in `card_location = 'hand'` (needed by `giveTheCards()` `checkCardsInHand()`, `TichuBot::pickThreeCardsToGive()`, and `race-tests.php#L1077`). On the client, `notif_unpassCards` restores the 3 cards into `this.gamedatas.hand` (14 cards) **and** pre-slots `passSlots[0..2].givenCard` as an editable local draft in `GameModel` (matching `[prev, partner, next]` server order): clicking a single slot swaps one mis-clicked card; clicking **Reset choices** empties all slots; clicking **Pass selected cards** re-confirms in 1 click. When `reopenedByTichu === true`, `<tichu-pass-area>` displays a non-blocking banner (`"Tichu called by <Caller> — review or confirm your pass"`). If a player refreshes (`F5`) while **already submitted and waiting** in `ST_GIVE_CARDS`, `getAllDatas()` privately populates `givenCards` for `$currentPlayerId` so `<tichu-pass-area>` shows their locked submitted cards (unclickable) alongside the **Undo Pass** button.

4. **Received-Card Origin Badges, Seat Orientation & Lifecycle (`Papayoo` pattern)**:
   - **Recommended**: Pair **Papayoo**'s 5s `.receivedCard` border/pulse highlight on `finishAcceptCards()` with a subtle persistent corner badge (`L` / `P` / `R`) and localized tooltip (`"Passed by Partner (<name>)"`, `"Passed from Left (<name>)"`, `"Passed from Right (<name>)"`) on `<tichu-card>` rendered inside `<tichu-hand>` only (`showReceivedBadges` prop). Derive visual Left/Right dynamically from `card.passed_from` using [`getNextPlayersDesc()`](file:///Users/brohlfs/git/tichu/src/elements/game-model.ts#L219-L230) (`dir 1 = playersDesc[0]` = prev in turn order; `dir 2 = playersDesc[1]` = partner; `dir 3 = playersDesc[2]` = next in turn order) and `GameModel.clockwise$` matching [`tichu-pass-area.ts#L40-L42`](file:///Users/brohlfs/git/tichu/src/elements/tichu-pass-area.ts#L40-L42) & [`tichu-last-played.ts#L94-L107`](file:///Users/brohlfs/git/tichu/src/elements/tichu-last-played.ts#L94-L107):
     - `!clockwise` (counterclockwise): `dir 1` (prev) -> **Left (`L`)**, `dir 2` -> **Partner (`P`)**, `dir 3` (next) -> **Right (`R`)**.
     - `clockwise`: `dir 3` (next) -> **Left (`L`)**, `dir 2` -> **Partner (`P`)**, `dir 1` (prev) -> **Right (`R`)**.
   - **No-Go UI Rules**: Never bind `style=` onto `<tichu-card>` from `<tichu-stock>` (wipes CSS vars set in `TichuCard::render()`); never apply `filter: drop-shadow` (`.dj_safari` GPU lag); never show origin badges on `<tichu-last-played>` or `<tichu-trick-modal>`.
